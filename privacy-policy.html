Privacy Policy – TikToker Panel

Last updated: November 16, 2026

TikToker Panel ("the App") is a personal desktop application used by its owner to manage video files and upload them to the owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Information we access

When you log in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and refresh token that allow it to upload videos to your account on your behalf. The App may also read basic profile information (such as your username) that TikTok provides.

2. How information is stored

All data, including tokens, settings, video lists and logs, is stored locally on your own computer. The App does not send this data to any server operated by us, and we do not collect, sell, rent or share personal data with third parties.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to upload the videos you select to your own TikTok account and to display the status of those uploads.

4. Third parties

Data sent to TikTok is handled under TikTok's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the App's local database and settings files. You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services.
